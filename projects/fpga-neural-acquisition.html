FPGA / SoC research · In development

FPGA-Based Neural Acquisition — Rev-2

Developing neural acquisition interfaces using Verilog, Zynq-7000 and Cyclone IV, with chip emulators, timestamped capture, AXI DMA, and embedded Linux bring-up.

Architecture and validation

Developed Intan-compatible SPI control and FPGA chip emulators to test acquisition interfaces before integrating the target devices. Demonstrated three board-to-board links with corresponding chip emulators; system development remains ongoing.

Acquisition data path

Integrated timestamped records, FIFOs, and AXI DMA into a Zynq prototype. Used simulation testbenches, Vitis test applications, UART diagnostics, and record-integrity checks to investigate hardware/software interfaces.

Embedded Linux bring-up

Built and booted a custom PetaLinux image on a Zynq-7010 board with UART console access and Ethernet connectivity. Used Vivado and Quartus for synthesis, implementation, pin constraints, timing-report analysis, and JTAG programming.

Current research direction

Investigating compact wireless acquisition with MCU-based transport and a possible low-power FPGA interface. Planned evaluation compares sustained throughput, dropped samples, timing reliability, and measured power consumption. A day of battery life is a design target, not a demonstrated result.
